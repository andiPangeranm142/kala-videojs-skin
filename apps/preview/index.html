Kala

An observatory player for the videos you watch in the dark.

Kala — Sanskrit & Indonesian for time. A Video.js v10 skin that treats every video as a night sky: the timeline is a horizon, chapters are stars, and progress is an aurora rolling across it. Built for slow cinema, space documentaries, and night-sky films — the kind of video you watch with the lights off.

01 · The concept

Why an observatory?

Most players are neutral chrome. Kala picks a niche — the night-sky film — and commits to it, so every detail has a reason to exist.

Time, made visible

The Night Arc turns the timeline into a horizon line. Chapter stars mark the moments that matter, the moon thumb glows as it travels, and the live chapter line under the title always tells you where in the night you are.

Cinema for the dark

A floating glass console, an eclipse play button, aurora progress. The chrome is designed to sit over footage — bright launch skies or black space — without ever shouting over it.

An instrument, not a decoration

Every control is keyboard-operable, labelled, and reachable by touch. The shortcuts guide is one ? away, and the skin stays readable with reduced motion, high contrast, and forced colors.

02 · Anatomy

Everything in the frame earns its place.

The Night Arc

A horizon-line seek bar with an aurora gradient fill, a glowing moon thumb, and a buffered shimmer. It swells under your pointer; the hover card shows a live thumbnail, time, and chapter.

Chapter stars

Every chapter boundary is a twinkling star on the arc. Hover one to preview its moment; the title block names the chapter you are in.

The Eclipse

The big play button is a glowing ring with a breathing halo while paused, flanked by ±10s seek buttons. Double-tap the sides to seek; double-tap the middle for fullscreen.

Inline volume

The volume slider unfurls from the mute button on hover or focus, with a live percentage pill. On touch it stays open where there is room.

Full settings menu

Quality (with live HLS renditions), audio tracks, playback speed, and captions — in a glass menu with submenus, all keyboard navigable.

Keyboard field guide

Press ? or the keyboard button for the full shortcut map — play, seek, volume, fullscreen, PiP, captions, speed, and jump-to-percentage keys.

Three skies

Midnight, Aurora, and Ember re-tint the whole aurora live — the gradient, the thumb glow, and the ambient light around the player.

Feedback, everywhere

Glass pills confirm volume, seek, fullscreen, PiP, and caption changes; the orbiting-dot spinner marks buffering; screen-reader announcements narrate state.

03 · Skies

One player, three night skies.

Pick a sky — the hero player above changes with it. The skin also honours the public --media-accent-color property, so any site can re-tint it.

07 · Accessibility

Built to be used by everyone.

  • Keyboard-first. Tab order follows the visual layout; the amber focus ring carries a dark halo so it reads on bright and dark frames.
  • Screen-reader friendly. Every control is labelled, sliders expose their values, and a live region announces play, pause, seek, volume, and fullscreen changes.
  • Contrast, tested both ways. The console, scrims, and text shadows hold up over the bright Daylight footage and the black of space.
  • Touch targets. 44 px buttons, a tall seek hit-area, and double-tap gestures for seek and fullscreen.
  • Reduced motion. The halo, twinkle, and ambient drift all stop when prefers-reduced-motion is set.
  • Forced colors & high contrast. Dedicated styles keep every control legible in Windows High Contrast and with reduced transparency.
